# Review governance, 2026-11-07 batch

Do not supply this file, old score reports or the user-requested target to formal graders. The scoring protocol and required evidence are shared; the acceptance decision is made only after independent reports are collected.

## Acceptance

The user's target is at least 9.5 in every applicable product dimension P1–P6 and method dimension M1–M5 from each of Terra, Gemini 3.8 Flash and Kimi K2.8, with no unresolved hard failure or material issue. All required evidence must be directly inspected by each grader. An insufficient-evidence dimension prevents an overall score and acceptance. Weighted averages cannot compensate for a weak dimension; rounding cannot pass a threshold. No grader is required to assign the target.

This is a scoped engineering/expert review, not acceptance of current provider integrations, other browsers or real-user usability. Those remain explicitly unknown when untested. An overall claim that the whole released product is 9.5 is not supported by model review alone.

## Budget and independence

One draft-calibration batch plus at most one clarification/recalibration, then a frozen protocol. At most three product candidates and one final independent challenge set per candidate. A used/revealed challenge becomes a regression, not a fresh holdout. A grader that fails safety calibration or cannot inspect required evidence is provisional; do not replace it with another model while retaining its name. Record the actual model route and evidence access limits.

Each formal score comes from a fresh context without prior history, target, candidate rank or other reports. Give every grader the same version-bound evidence index with mandatory evidence IDs. Never send a persuasive improvement narrative in place of a missing artifact. Collect reports before sharing findings. A relabelled repeat calibration item measures only within-batch judgment stability, not statistical reliability.

The method auditor and implementation agents do not grade their own work. The independent challenge author seals specifications before product edits. If a technical author fails, record it and replace the author, not the challenge outcome. A failed run remains visible. No repeated sampling until a preferred score appears.

## Honest stop

Stop when all conditions pass or after three candidates/two no-improvement candidates. If external access, user observation, model calibration or direct evidence prevents acceptance, report the specific unmet conditions and useful completed changes. Do not enlarge scope, lower the bar, hide failures or claim success. A later authorized task or new external evidence can open a new batch.
